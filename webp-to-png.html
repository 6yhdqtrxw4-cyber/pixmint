FAQ

Will the PNG be bigger than the WebP?

Usually yes. WebP is a more efficient codec, so a PNG of the same image is typically several times larger. That's the price of universal compatibility.

Is transparency kept?

Yes. PNG supports full alpha transparency and the conversion preserves it — logos and stickers keep their transparent backgrounds.

Does converting lose quality?

PNG is lossless, so the converted image keeps exactly the quality of the WebP you started with — no additional degradation.

Animated WebP?

The converter exports the first frame as a static PNG. Animated conversion is on the roadmap.

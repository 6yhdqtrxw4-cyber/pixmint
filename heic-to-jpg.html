FAQ

Will my JPGs be bigger than the HEICs?

Usually yes — HEIC is roughly half the size of an equivalent JPG, that's why Apple uses it. A quality-matched JPG is typically about 2x the HEIC file size. Lower the quality slider if you need smaller files.

Does it work on Windows and Android?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari), on any OS, because all decoding happens in the browser itself.

Is EXIF data (date, location) preserved?

Conversion re-encodes the image; most metadata is stripped in the process. That is usually a privacy win — your JPGs won't leak GPS coordinates when you share them.

Is there a file count or size limit?

No hard limit — you're limited only by your device's memory. Dozens of photos per batch work fine on modern phones and laptops.
